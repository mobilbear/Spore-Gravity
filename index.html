Stage: Stage 1

Stage Overview

Guide a single cell, gather food, and evolve speed, sensing, or resilience.

  • Food
  • Hazard / prey
  • Player organism
  • Vision radius

Controls

  • Move: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Toggle Evolution Menu: E or button
  • Restart stage: button in top bar
  • Switch stage: dropdown in top bar

Progression Notes

Early traits cascade into later stages: mobility, perception, and diet leanings will eventually influence pack behavior, resource efficiency, and technology. Stages 3 and 4 are scaffolded with TODOs so you can extend the simulation.

Evolution Upgrades

Spend points earned from food to improve your organism.

Single-player prototype. Runs entirely in your browser.
